The match

Every touch is physics.

The ball curls, dips, skids and bounces like a real one. Players have weight and momentum, so first touches, passes and shots can go wrong under pressure. A joystick and four buttons on your phone, or the keyboard on a computer.

  • Hold to weight a pass, slide up to lift it
  • Free kicks and penalties from behind the taker
  • Goal replays, a walk-out from the tunnel and a crowd that roars

Your club

Open packs. Pick your eleven.

Every player card has twelve stats, plus height and weight, and all of them change how he plays. Open packs to sign new players, choose a formation and drag them into place.

  • A free daily pack, then Bronze, Silver, Gold and Legend
  • Six formations on a drag-and-drop tactics board
  • Win coins for wins, draws and goals

Club studio

Your colours, your crest.

Design the shirt, build a crest from shapes, emblems and lettering, and watch your fans fly it on flags, banners and a giant tifo before kick-off.

  • Ten shirt designs in any colours
  • Crest maker with nine shapes and fourteen emblems
  • Six grounds, from a classic old ground to an Italian bowl

Get the game.

GameNight runs in your web browser, so there's nothing to download from a store. Open it and play, or add it to your home screen so it opens full screen like any other app.

Computer

Open the gameChrome or Edge
  1. Open the game in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the install icon at the right of the address bar, or the menu, then Install GameNight.
  3. It now opens in its own window from your desktop or Start menu.

Play with the keyboard: WASD to move, J, K and L to pass, play through and shoot.

Android

Open the gameChrome
  1. Open the game in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Start it from your home screen and tap once to go full screen and sideways.

Hold your phone sideways for the best view.

iPhone

Open the gameSafari
  1. Open the game in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Start it from your home screen and turn the phone sideways.

Works on iPad too.
